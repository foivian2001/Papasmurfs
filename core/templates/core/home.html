What we do

One platform for competitive progression.

Papasmurfs is a Greece-based gaming service focused on competitive progression and account development. Instead of forcing customers through a single generic service, our catalogue is built around each game's own ranking or progression system. League of Legends players can select Solo/Duo progression, CS2 players can move through competitive ranks, Fortnite customers can choose between Build and Zero Build progression, while GTA Online players can focus on RP and character levels.

Our speciality

Built around the games we understand.

Papasmurfs concentrates on a focused selection of competitive games rather than trying to cover every title available. Each service is structured around the progression system of its game, allowing the catalogue to provide meaningful rank routes, pricing and estimated completion times.

Our team operates from Greece while the service itself is entirely online, making the platform accessible to players regardless of where they play from.
